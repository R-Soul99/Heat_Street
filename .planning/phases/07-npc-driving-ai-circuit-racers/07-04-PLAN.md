<objective>
Vertical slice 4: a crashed, flipped or wedged AI racer gets itself back into the race. First it drives out, then it resets onto the road at its last checkpoint and pays the same time cost as a player respawn.

Purpose: SC3 and D-13. It also consolidates the checkpoint reset-pose math into one pure helper and verifies its heading convention. `poseForCheckpoint` currently derives heading as a bearing `atan2(dz, dx)` but hands it to `resetPose`, which treats it as a Y-yaw (forward = (-sin h, -cos h)). This task must prove or fix that before AI resets depend on it.
Output: `checkpoint-pose.ts`, `ai-stuck-detector.ts`, `TransformCache.snapBody`, recovery wired into the Circuit Race coordinator, and the player's body registered as an AI avoidance obstacle.
</objective>
