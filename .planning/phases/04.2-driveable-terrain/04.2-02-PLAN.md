Add `createPaintLookup(colliderHandle: number, paint: PaintMap, fallback: SurfaceType): SurfacePaintLookup` that closes over the paint map and calls `paintIndexAt` + `decodePaintSurface` with no per-call allocation.

Use `=== undefined` / `=== null` checks, never truthiness (handle 0 is valid). Do not change anything else in the tick.

Write `tests/terrain-surface.test.ts` in the style of tests/surface.test.ts: flat ground collider (cuboid via buildTelemetryScene or a flat heightfield), register its handle in a paint lookup over a two-surface paint map, spawn the car straddling x = 0, settle 30 ticks with neutral input, assert per-wheel surfaces.
