    1. Open http://localhost:5173/tools/terrain-editor/index.html. Press New..., name demo-hills, size about 600 x 600 m, cell size 3 m (or 2 m), Create. You should see a flat grass area and the default hint.
    2. Right-drag to orbit, middle-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, press T for top-down and T again to return.
    3. Sculpt a few hills with raise/lower (H; Alt-drag lowers), then smooth (G) and flatten (F) a spot. Try to make a spike: the ring should turn amber and the slope should stop at the max slope.
    4. Place a wedge stamp (W) as an earth ramp and a mound.
    5. Lay a road loop with the road brush (R) over the hills, at least one road crossing another (should make a junction at the same height), one Highway-class bend (banked), and paint a mud or gravel patch (P).
    6. Erase part of one road (E): paint and road go, the levelled ground stays. Undo (Ctrl+Z) brings it back; redo (Ctrl+Y) removes it again.
    7. Save terrain (Ctrl+S). The status shows the saved size. Reload the page, Load demo-hills: everything is as saved and undo history is empty.
    8. Put the cursor on a road and press Drive here (D). The game opens in a new tab on that spot. Drive the loop: smooth over hills, no jitter, mud/gravel feels different, skid marks appear on the terrain.
    9. With the game tab still on ?debug, note the profiler HUD (frame ms, physics ms, render ms, draw calls, triangles, bodies) while driving over the busiest view. Repeat on http://localhost:5173/?map=juliette-ga&debug and http://localhost:5173/?map=feel-test&debug.
