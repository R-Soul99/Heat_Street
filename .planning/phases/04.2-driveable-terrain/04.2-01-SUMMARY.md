# Phase 04.2 Plan 01: Terrain grid and paint map Summary

Triangle-consistent height sampler proven against Rapier raycasts at 200 seeded points (diagonal measured as `row0col1-row1col0`), plus a one-byte-per-pixel surface paint map with a road flag.

## Measured diagonal

`TERRAIN_DIAGONAL = "row0col1-row1col0"`. My initial assumption (`row0col0-row1col1`) was wrong; the test showed 2.2 m worst error for it and 6e-6 m for the opposite. The test also asserts the wrong diagonal disagrees by more than 1e-2 m.

## Commits

- 1396d8f feat(04.2-01): terrain grid with Rapier-proved triangle sampler
- 7dad950 feat(04.2-01): surface paint map with road flag and per-position lookup

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Catmull-Rom edge handling.** Plan said "clamped-edge", but clamped neighbours do not reproduce linear data in edge cells, contradicting the plane-reproduction requirement. Used linear ghost-point extrapolation at edges instead. Originals are preserved exactly.

Otherwise the plan was executed as written. `MAX_SLOPE_DEG` and `DEFAULT_MAX_BANK_DEG` remain provisional until the plan 06 gate. Mitigations T-04.2-01 (size RangeErrors before allocating) and T-04.2-02 (out-of-enum fallback) are implemented and tested.
