# Phase 04.2 Plan 09: Road brush core Summary

Road brush as pure core: one stroke paints the surface, levels and smooths heights (with automatic banking) and writes the centreline as graph edges, returned as one undoable `StrokeDiff`; erase removes paint and graph edge but leaves terrain.

## What was built

- `ROAD_PRESETS` (street/highway/track/dirt), `prepareStrokePolyline` (resample at cell/2, Chaikin x2, hand-written RDP 0.25 m), `roadHeightProfile` (moving-average low-pass, pin blending, forward/backward grade clamp), `roadBankAngles` (3-point circumradius, atan(v^2/gR) capped, 20 m ramp in and out, zero for streets/dirt).
- `roadLevelRadiusM = max(halfWidth, cell*sqrt2)`, `levelRoadBand` (core level + smoothstep shoulder, protect mask, returns mask/window/maxDelta), `roadVertexMaskFromPaint`.
- `applyRoadStroke`: prepare, `findStrokeCrossings` (endpoints moved onto joins, vertices inserted at crossings, heights pinned: cross/snap-edge from terrain, snap-node from node y), densify, protect mask, profile, banking (tapered to zero near joins), level, paint, masked `limitSlope` (warning "Slope limit reached (35 degrees)..." on road violations), `addRoadStroke` with y read back from terrain, default spawn, node-y resync, graph snapshots into the recorder. Width clamped 3..20 m.
- `eraseRoad`: road paint back to ground surface, `eraseGraphDisc`, heights untouched.
- 22 tests in `tests/terrain-road.test.ts`, including coarse 8 m cells, T junction, reused node, crossing, parallel road bit-identical, replace, undo exact, validator round trip.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 2 - Correctness] Stronger shoulder protection.** With 8 m cells the levelled radius exceeds the painted half-width, so paint alone would let a later shoulder blend alter an earlier road's levelled triangles. The protect mask also covers the levelled radius around existing graph edges. The limiter mask follows the plan (paint + new band).
2. **[Gate finding applied] Vertical curve length.** The plan left it as a 15 m moving average; `applyRoadStroke` passes `smoothM = clamp(2.4 s * design speed, 15, 80)` (highway 67 m, street 27 m).
3. **[Rule 1 - Bug] Layering regex.** A parameter named `window` tripped the `window.` purity check in `tests/layering.test.ts`; renamed.
4. **Bank ramp.** Bank smoothing pads the ends with zero samples instead of shrinking the window, so the bank ramps in from the stroke ends.
5. **Single commit.** Both tasks live in one source file written together, so tasks 1 and 2 were committed together (05a9556) rather than separately; tests were run per task scope before the commit.
6. **Junction y resync.** After `addRoadStroke`, nodes near the stroke have y (and incident edge endpoint y) snapped to terrain to guarantee D-16 despite interpolated junction y.
