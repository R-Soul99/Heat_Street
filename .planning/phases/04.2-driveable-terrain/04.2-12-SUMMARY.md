## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug in plan spec] Bounds for 600x400 at 3 m cells**
- The acceptance example expects bounds -200..200 for depth 400 m at 3 m cells and also "equal to the grid extent". Cells are square and the format derives scale from cellSizeM, so 400 m is not representable (133 rows = 399 m). I kept the stronger invariant (bounds equal the real grid extent: -199.5..199.5) and snapped sizes to whole cells. The test asserts -199.5 for 600x400 and exactly +-201 for 600x402. Documented in new-area.ts.

**2. Drive here is disabled.** No game-side terrain loader exists yet; the button is rendered (primary style) with a title noting it is wired later.

**3. Cursor ring** is a 1px LineLoop (WebGL ignores line width), draped on the terrain heights, depthTest off; the spec's 2px is not achievable with core line primitives.
