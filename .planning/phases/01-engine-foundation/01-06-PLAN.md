<objective>
Build the debug gate and the profiler HUD: the `?debug` convention, a single-key overlay toggle, and a DOM overlay reporting every metric ROADMAP SC4 requires, each checked against the written frame budget.

Purpose: SC4 requires a profiler HUD toggled by a single key showing physics ms, draw calls, triangles and body count against a written frame budget. D-03 adds render ms and states that "minimal" still means all of them, not fewer. D-04 fixes the budget at 16.6 ms total with physics at or under 4 ms and requires it written down so the HUD is checked against it rather than against an implicit assumption. D-05 establishes the `?debug` plus hotkey convention that Phase 2's lil-gui tuning panel will reuse - the export shape here matters more than the implementation.

Output: `src/core/frame-stats.ts`, `src/debug/debug-gate.ts`, `src/debug/profiler-hud.ts` and two test files.
</objective>
