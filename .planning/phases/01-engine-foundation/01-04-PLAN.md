  <behavior>
    - Construction primes both buffers from the live bodies, so `prev` deep-equals `cur` immediately after construction and frame 1 never lerps from zeros
    - Buffer length is `bodies.length * 7` for both `prev` and `cur`, and both are `Float64Array`
    - Layout is px, py, pz, qx, qy, qz, qw at offset `index * 7` - reading a body's translation and rotation back out of `cur` matches Rapier's values exactly
    - `captureAsPrevious()` copies `cur` into `prev` without re-reading Rapier: mutating a body then calling `captureAsPrevious()` leaves `prev` equal to the pre-mutation `cur`
    - `captureAsCurrent()` re-reads every body, so after a `world.step()` the `cur` buffer differs from `prev` for any moving body
    - Quaternion components round-trip: `qw` is never left at 0 for a body with identity rotation
    - The cache allocates no new arrays after construction: calling `captureAsPrevious` and `captureAsCurrent` 1000 times does not change `prev.byteLength` or `cur.byteLength`
  </behavior>
  <action>
    Create `src/physics/transform-cache.ts` following RESEARCH.md "Code Examples > Example 3" structurally.

    Export `XFORM_STRIDE = 7` as a named constant and use it everywhere rather than a bare `7`.

    Store `prev` and `cur` as two `Float64Array` buffers of `bodies.length * XFORM_STRIDE`, keyed by dense body index. RESEARCH.md is explicit that this shape should be committed to now even though Phase 1 has seven bodies, because Phase 7 and 8 pursuer counts are the reason it exists. Do not store objects.

    `captureAsPrevious()` is `this.prev.set(this.cur)` - a buffer copy, never a re-read from Rapier. Getting this backwards inverts interpolation and produces motion that runs one tick behind reality.

    `captureAsCurrent()` loops the bodies, reads `b.translation()` (a `Vector` with x, y, z) and `b.rotation()` (a `Rotation` with x, y, z, w), and writes the seven components at `i * XFORM_STRIDE`.

    The constructor calls `captureAsCurrent()` then `this.prev.set(this.cur)` so both buffers are primed.

    Expose `prev` and `cur` as readonly public fields. Per PATTERNS.md's placement decision, this class does NOT apply transforms to meshes and does NOT import `three`; `src/render/interpolator.ts` in plan 01-05 reads these buffers. That keeps `src/physics/` free of `three`.
