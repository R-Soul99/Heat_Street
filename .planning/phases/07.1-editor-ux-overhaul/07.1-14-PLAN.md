<objective>
User feedback (checkpoint, 2026-11-11): "road centre is the line, amount of tarmac either side is the width, and if there's a kink just make sure there's enough tarmac to cover it (even if it overlaps)". Today the ribbon builder offsets left/right rails by width/2 (miter-joined, clamped, D-P10). At a kink shorter than half-width the inner rail backtracks, its quads turn inside out (down-facing triangles = visible hole from above and a down-facing collider face), and the validator reports GEOMETRY_SELF_INTERSECTION. The earlier workaround (tools/map-editor/road-check.ts fixFoldedRoads) deletes the user's points; the user rejected that. Fix the builder instead.
</objective>
