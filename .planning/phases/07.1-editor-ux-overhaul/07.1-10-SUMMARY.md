# Phase 07.1 Plan 10: Grid select and edit feedback Summary

Grid select is on the rail (S) with a yellow persistent selection outline (cyan live drag box), a flyout with a selection read-out, large Raise/Lower selection buttons and a collapsible Step section (range 0.25-5 m plus stepper, shared value; arrows and [ ] step it, Shift = 1 m). Ctrl+drag with a selection pushes/pulls live (0.05 m/px, Shift 0.01) and commits one undo step on release; Esc, pointercancel, dialogs, tool change and area load cancel and restore. Clicking with no area now says to load or create one. The info bar `change` field shows `last +0.98 m` (plus held-by-roads count), road-held and slope-clamp results show in error style with how to fix, and Raise/Lower tooltips explain strength and zoom.
