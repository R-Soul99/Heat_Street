# Phase 07.1 Plan 15: Road problems panel Summary

A "Road problems" panel lists every road check failure in plain English, flies the camera to the one you click, and offers one-click fixes that each undo in one step.

## What was built

- road-problems.ts (pure): failures + stale unlocked roads -> rows {text, tip, blocking, fixKind?, hint?, target, at}. Blocking rows first. Category codes only in the tooltip. Locations: segment N / points[N] parsed from messages, steepest segment for gradient, edge midpoint by length, node position.
- Fixes offered per category:
  - Duplicate points: Remove duplicate points (end points never moved).
  - Steep road: Re-grade to the ground when unlocked and stale; Smooth the road's heights (graph heights only, ends kept, aims at 0.45 against the 0.5 ceiling) when locked; hand advice when unlocked and already matching the ground or when the ends are too far apart in height.
  - Stale unlocked road: Re-grade to the ground (existing regradeRoads path).
  - Length mismatch, referential integrity, directed one-way trap, cut-off pieces, self-intersection: no automatic fix, hand advice shown (the last two do not block saving).
- Panel: View > Road problems, refreshes (debounced) after edits, undo/redo and area load, opens automatically when a save is refused. Click row: camera flies there, red ring, beam and road outline for 4 s.
- Dispatcher `applyRoadFix` pushes one undo entry per fix.
