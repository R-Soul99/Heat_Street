## Behaviour notes
- blockWindowAt clips the block at grid edges (does not shift it); returns null off-grid or non-finite. blockCells rounds and clamps 1..8.
- raiseBlock clamps stepM 0.25..5, no-ops on non-finite input; relax off for sharp blocks.
- smoothRegion: strength 0..1, passes 1..10; heights outside the window are fixed boundary; tested on a 200 x 200 grid with bit-exact undo.
- Contours: vertex exactly on a level counts as above; saddles resolved by centre average; per-cell level cap of 64.
