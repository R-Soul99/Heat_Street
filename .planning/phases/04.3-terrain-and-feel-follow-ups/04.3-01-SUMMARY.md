## Lift-off baseline (unmodified vehicle.ts)

peakSlipDeg / headingOvershootDeg (degrees gained past release heading).

| Routine | defaultTuning() peak slip / overshoot | shipped car peak slip / overshoot |
|---|---|---|
| lift-off-60 | 17.7 / 94.0 | 29.8 / 94.6 |
| lift-straight-60 | 2.8 / 6.4 | 3.0 / 4.9 |
| lift-gentle-60 | 4.1 / 170.6 | 5.2 / 199.9 |
| lift-straight-90 | 9.3 / 95.3 | 13.5 / 116.9 |

Reading: peakSlipDeg alone does not show the spin. lift-gentle-60 and lift-straight-90 rotate 117 to 200 deg past the lift point while peak slip stays under 30 deg, so plan 05 should assert on headingOvershootDeg (and final heading), not peakSlipDeg. None of the three new routines exceeds peakSlipDeg 30 on the shipped car, but lift-gentle-60 (overshoot ~200 deg, final heading 235 deg) and lift-straight-90 (overshoot ~117 deg, final -113 deg) are clearly spin-like by heading. lift-straight-60 is stable (overshoot under 5 deg). lift-off-60 on the shipped car has peak slip 29.8, near the 30 line.
