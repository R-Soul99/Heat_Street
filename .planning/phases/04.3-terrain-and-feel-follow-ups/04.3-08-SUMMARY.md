## Contact-loss diagnosis

Current code (plan 05 shipped no reshape, so vehicle.ts is as at plan 01). 11 of 12 lanes pass with 0 ticks. One failure, identical to the plan 01 baseline:

| Lane | Tick | Wheel | Suspension lengths | vy | Throttle | rearSideFriction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| climb 15 m/s dir -1 | 2894 | 2 (single) | 0.27/0.28/**0.45**/0.26 | -0.55 | 0.01 | 0.277 |

Wheel 2 is at full droop (0.45 = rest length) for exactly one tick on the descent, with the car coasting.

Opt-in bisect (GATE_BISECT=1, skipped by default; climb 15 dir -1, loss ticks):

| Variation | Loss ticks |
|---|---|
| shipped (none) | 1 |
| grip, steerAtSpeed, turnIn, accel, topSpeed, pivot, pivotEase, slideSurge, tyreFalloff, regrip, flick each reverted to neutral | 1 |
| **liftOff = 0, 5 (shipped), 10** | **1, 1, 1** |
| lowGearPunch reverted to 5 / set 6.5 / set 7.5 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| looseness reverted to 5 / set 5.0 / set 5.6 | 0 / 0 / 1 |

Conclusions:
1. **The lift-off blend is NOT implicated.** liftOff 0, 5 and 10 give the same single lost tick. Lift-off oversteer does not cause this contact loss.
2. No single assist or feel term is "the offender". Any nudge to lowGearPunch (even 7.0 to 7.5, 6.5) or looseness (5.3 to 5.0) removes the tick, while 5.6 keeps it. That is a chaotic, marginal event: a rear wheel unloads for one tick at a vertical-curve crest at 15 m/s with ~0 throttle, and tiny drivetrain perturbations shift the trajectory off the crest tick. The cause is geometric/chaotic (ray reaches full droop 0.45 for one tick), not a specific term. Fix candidate (a) is unavailable (plan 05 parked); candidate (b) would be tuning a default to dodge one tick (overfitting, non-robust: the next change reintroduces a different lane, as already seen vs the 04.2-VERIFICATION expectation of hill 15 / climb 25).
3. Robust remedy is candidate (c): suspension droop / ray reach (more droop travel before the ray misses). That changes ride-height or settle feel and needs the plan 11 sign-off drive. Candidate (d) contact skin is ruled out by 04.2-GATE.
