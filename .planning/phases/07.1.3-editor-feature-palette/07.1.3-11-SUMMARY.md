## Human check
Approved after two rounds. First round found: building rotation with the wheel while dragging was unusable; picket and chainlink drew as solid walls; ramps not editable; no visible triangle readout; trees only selectable by their base; refused ghost not red; HUD not visible. Second round found ramps still unselectable. All fixed (see below); the user then replied "approved".

## First-run fixes (round one)
- Arrow keys turn a selected building, ramp, tree or bush (new pure `rotateSceneryItem`; a turn that swings a corner onto road is refused).
- Fences: picket is a backing rail plus a slat about every 0.8 m, chainlink is posts plus three wires; collider and sight rules unchanged; triangle estimate follows (`fenceRailBoxCount`).
- Ramps: Selected ramp panel with Height, Angle, Width (one undo step each, `updateRamp`).
- Triangle readout: permanent "Scenery" field in the info bar (the sidebar line could not be shown to be missing from code; cause unconfirmed).
- Picking: `pickFeatureByRay`.
- Ghost tint was multiplied by the instance colour; it now replaces it.
- HUD: it is off until Backquote is pressed under `?debug`. The yellow triangle behind the map dropdown was the road-direction arrow sitting at the top-left; it now starts hidden.

## Ramp pick root cause (round two)
The ramp pick only tested a 1.5 m circle around the ramp's centre, which is the START of its foot at ground level; the ramp runs rampLengthM (about 6 m by default) along its heading. The ray fix from round one did nothing for ramps because they were handled only by that ground test. Fixed with `rampFootprint` (oriented rectangle), used by the ray test, the ground fallback and the selection box. Tests pin a ray at the middle and at the top end.

## Ramps and crests are hard-coded grass (open follow-up)
The crest/ramp object format has no surface field. `buildCrestGeometry` stamps `surface: "grass"` on every crest and ramp, and the render palette and grip lookup follow it, so every ramp, including the authored feel-test ramps, is grass. Not changed here. A follow-up would need: an optional `surface` on `AuthoredCrest`; parse, validate and serialise it in terrain-format.ts (with a format-version decision for old files); `crest-geometry.ts` using `crest.surface ?? "grass"`; a Surface choice in the ramp placement sidebar and Selected ramp panel (default tarmac for palette ramps, one undo step per change); a check that the physics collider mapping reads the entry surface; tests. The user has not decided whether to do this; it is an open follow-up, not a decision.

## Deviations from Plan
1. Dense fixture fence mix changed to 1 post-rail, 3 picket, 6 chainlink lines (fence total still 7,560, worst case still 37,900) because slatted pickets cost 156 triangles per 8 m segment.
2. Editor.ts no longer calls `pickFeatureAt(` directly; picking goes through `pickFeatureByRay`, which falls back to it in features.ts.
3. Three differences from the UI-SPEC: a press inside a building (or on any pickable item) selects it, so Shift is needed to start a box selection over scenery; the bulk-delete confirm uses `openDialog` with "Keep features" listed first for initial focus, so the confirm button is not danger-styled; the building drag ghost uses the palette style's wall colour, not the building's own style.
4. A sidecar test was made independent of the saved demo-hills file, which gained scenery from the editor.
