A miss deselects and falls through to the existing 07.1.2 box selection. Delete with a selected scenery item removes it (one step) and is routed before the TERRAIN_HOTKEYS lookup; Delete with no selected item keeps deleting roads. No road or extent maths in editor.ts; the decision is moveSceneryItem's.
    Bulk removal per the plan 02 decision (default: sidebar button "Delete scenery in selection" in the Select sidebar, enabled when a box selection exists): compute `removeInMask` with `CellMask.cellAtXZ`; above 20 items open `confirmDialog` with heading "Delete {n} features?", body "They are removed from this map. If the user chose the Delete-key alternative in plan 02, route Delete to remove both roads and scenery in the box, still as one undo step per part set.
    Selection highlight clears when the item disappears after undo/redo. Features re-seat after sculpts (wired in plan 09) and building seats follow the dispatcher re-seat from plan 07.
