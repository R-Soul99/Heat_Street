# Phase 07.1.3 Plan 10: Hedge, fence and ramp placement Summary

Hedges, fences and ramps can now be placed from the terrain editor palette with previews, refusals and one undo step each; all decisions come from the plan 07 pure modules and editor.ts only routes and draws.

## Commits
- 57c9315: hedge/fence lines and ramps (Tasks 1 and 2 in one commit; see Deviations)

## What was built
- Hedge/Fence sidebar: variant swatches (plan 09 builder and column rule), Height (min from the catalog variant, max 6, step 0.1, default from the catalog; picking a variant resets Height and rebuilds the sidebar), running "{length} m, {k} points" readout, and the plan 09 triangle readout and message line.
- Line drawing: clicks call `createLineBuilder().add`; accent dots, polyline and a ghost segment to the cursor, red when `canPlaceLine` refuses that segment. A refused click adds nothing.
- Enter or double-click finishes: `addLineFeature`, then `checkSceneryBudget`; refusal commits nothing, keeps the line in progress and writes the reason to the sidebar message line and the info bar. Success is one `commitScenery({ features })` step. Esc cancels, Backspace removes the last point. Enter/Backspace are handled only while a line is in progress, after the text-focus guard and before `TERRAIN_HOTKEYS`, and are not in that table. Undo/redo are ignored while a line is in progress. Switching kind or area cancels the line.
- Ramp sidebar: Height, Angle, Width steppers and a direction readout. Press sets the foot position, drag sets direction (accent arrow plus a translucent ghost from `buildCrestMeshes`), release calls `addRampObject` then `commitScenery({ objects })`. `canPlaceRampAt` refuses outside the area; roads are allowed. Esc, pointercancel, pointerleave and dialogs cancel the ramp gesture through the existing `cancelSceneryGesture`.
- All crests/ramps in the area are drawn with `buildCrestMeshes(crestsFromObjects(area.objects), ...)` in one group, rebuilt on install, on `objectsChanged` (apply, undo, redo) and after each height edit when the area has any crest objects. Ramps do not count toward the scenery triangle limit. `crest-geometry.ts` is unchanged.

## Deviations from Plan
1. Both tasks went into one commit (57c9315), because the shared routing, ghost and sidebar edits interleave in editor.ts and could not be split cleanly after the fact.
2. Ramps are rebuilt after every height edit when any crest object exists (not only when a stroke touches a ramp), since a ramp's extent is up to the foot radius plus length; the cost is small (a few ramp meshes).
3. Because the editor now draws all crest objects, existing dome crests in a loaded map also become visible in the editor (they were not drawn before).
4. WebGL draws 1 px lines, so the "2px accent polyline" of the UI-SPEC is 1 px; dots are 6 px as specified.
5. Hedge/fence swatch glyphs: the plan 09 swatch builder is reused with glyphs for hedge and fence.
