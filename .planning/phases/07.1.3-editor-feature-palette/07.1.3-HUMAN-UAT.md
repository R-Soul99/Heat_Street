## Tests

These cover the nine code-review fixes committed after the plan 11 human check was approved.
None of them has been run in a browser.

### 1. Arrow-key rotation of a selected item
expected: With a building, tree, bush or ramp selected, Left/Right turns it 15 degrees per press and Shift+Left/Right turns it 1 degree. Holding the key keeps turning. One hold, or a quick burst of taps, undoes as a single step. The sidebar Step value never changes.
result: pass

### 2. Building selection identity and legacy building boxes
expected: A palette-placed building can be selected, moved, turned and deleted. After Ctrl+Z the selection follows the same building or clears; it never jumps to a different building. On grid-test, the buildings that come from the model file cannot be selected, moved or deleted in the editor.
result: pass

### 3. Footprint and bounds refusals
expected: Dragging or turning a building so any part of its footprint crosses road paint is refused with the road reason. A ramp whose body would leave the map area is refused when placed, moved or turned.
result: pass

### 4. Esc from a Place tool
expected: Pressing Esc while a Place kind is armed returns to Select mode, not to the previously used brush, so the next click does not sculpt.
result: pass

### 5. Hedges and fences on a slope
expected: A hedge or fence drawn across sloping ground follows the ground at both ends of each segment. Driving into it on the slope, the car is stopped where the fence is drawn, with no gap underneath and no invisible wall above.
result: pass
