  <behavior>
    - P2P marks any unvisited checkpoint complete regardless of order, including a checkpoint that is not the current nearest-target suggestion, then selects the nearest remaining checkpoint by road path cost and completes after all checkpoints are visited.
    - Circuit rejects out-of-order checkpoints, advances the authored sequence, increments lap only after the final checkpoint, and completes after exactly three laps.
    - Hits are idempotent; snapshots expose current target, visited IDs, lap/total laps, completion, wrong-way state, and last-checkpoint respawn anchor.

Define wrong-way as an explicit ordered-mode state based on the car's current road progress/heading relative to the next sequence segment, with a stable threshold and hysteresis documented as constants; P2P must not show wrong-way merely because the player chooses an alternate legal route. Store a flat `RESPAWN_PENALTY_SEC = 5` default with a short rationale in the module, preserve the monotonic simulation clock, and expose reset/respawn transitions for the integration plan.
