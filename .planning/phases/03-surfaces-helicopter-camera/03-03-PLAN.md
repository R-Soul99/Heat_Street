<objective>
Make the ground under each tire change how that tire grips — the mechanism SURF-01
is entirely about.

Purpose: this is the first half of the phase's first vertical slice. After this plan,
a wheel standing on a mud collider is solved with mud's friction values and a wheel
standing on tarmac is solved with tarmac's, per wheel, per tick. There is no scene to
drive it on yet (plan 03-05) and nothing on screen (plan 03-06) — but the capability
is real and provable in a headless Rapier world.

Output: `src/physics/surface.ts`, a modified `src/physics/vehicle.ts`, and
`tests/surface.test.ts`.
</objective>
