<objective>
The editor's pure route-editing model (brief stage 6.1, 6.5, 6.6; D-09, D-11): stage-draft and route-draft operations with caps and warnings, and the `stages`/`routes` parts of the undo diff on the shared undo stack.

Output: `tools/terrain-editor/stages.ts`, `tools/terrain-editor/route-ops.ts`, undo diff extension, dispatcher commits, tests.

Decisions recorded (planner, user asleep):
- Stages and routes live in their own files, not the terrain sidecar; the editor keeps them as `area.stages` (StageDraft[]) and `area.routes` (RouteDraft[]) on the same `UndoTarget` so one 50-deep undo stack covers terrain, scenery and routes (research Pattern 6). `serializeTerrainSidecar` builds explicit fields, so these never leak into the sidecar (tested).
- Ids: stages `stage-<n>`, gates `g<n>` (unique within a stage), routes `<areaId>-route-<n>`, checkpoints `<routeId>-cp-<n>`, finish `<routeId>-finish`. Seeds = max numeric suffix + 1, kept in editor state, never in the file (copy `startIdSeed`).
- Warning threshold 600 m between consecutive items (brief 6.6); slope warning uses the editor's slope limit passed in by the caller.
- Click without drag: heading = previous item's heading, or toward the cursor from the previous item when that is at least 1 m away (fallbackGateHeading).
</objective>
