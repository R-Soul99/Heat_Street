## Decisions Made
- Lowered `TARGET_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` to 6m per the plan's arithmetic: nearest heightfield grid node at most ~30m away (128 cells over ~5.5km bounds), contributing at most `2 * smoothstep(30/130)` ≈ 0.27m of relief, plus `HEIGHTFIELD_SINK_M` (0.1m) = ~0.37m worst-case delta. Over 6m that's ~3.5deg; over the building-clamped 3m floor it's ~7deg — both comfortably under the DEM-era 12.9deg median / 26.9deg p95.
- Left `MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` (3) and `SHOULDER_BUILDING_SAFETY_MARGIN_M` (2) unchanged, as directed.
- Widened the worst-case-grade test's epsilon from the plan's literal `1e-9` to `1e-6` (see Deviations below) — a necessary correction to make the test physically meaningful under `Float32Array` storage, not a weakening of the gate itself (the gate is still the 15-degree grade comparison, which is exact arithmetic, not float-precision-sensitive).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Widened float32 storage epsilon in the worst-case-grade test**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (writing the flat-terrain grade regression tests)
- **Issue:** The plan's literal instruction was `Math.abs(y) <= 0.37 + 1e-9`. `ShoulderGeometryEntry.positions` is a `Float32Array`; storing the test's `-0.37` literal alone (before any geometry arithmetic) rounds to `-0.3700000047683716`, a ~4.8e-9 deviation from the float64 literal — already larger than the plan's `1e-9` tolerance, so the test failed on storage precision alone with zero actual defect in the geometry.
- **Fix:** Widened the tolerance to `WORST_CASE_DELTA_M + 1e-6`, comfortably above the float32 rounding error while still tight enough that any real geometry bug (e.g. a stray lerp between paved and outer rail height) would still fail the test. Documented inline with the measured float32 delta.
- **Files modified:** `tests/road-geometry.test.ts`
- **Verification:** `npx vitest run tests/road-geometry.test.ts tests/shoulder-clearance.test.ts` — 43/43 green after the fix; re-verified the test still fails when `TARGET_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` is temporarily reverted to 20 (see below).
- **Committed in:** `979ae06` (Task 2 commit)
