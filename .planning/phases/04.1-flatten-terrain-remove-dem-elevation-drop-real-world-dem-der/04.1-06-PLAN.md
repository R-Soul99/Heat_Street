<objective>
Make the compiled map's elevation provenance honest now that no DEM is used (RESEARCH.md
Pitfall 1, Open Question 2), and bump the compiler version so a stale DEM-era artifact is
detectable.

Purpose: `source.demSource` and `attribution.dem` exist for exactly one reason — ADR 0001's
legal/licensing traceability. A shipped map claiming `usgs-3dep-1m` provenance for terrain that
was hand-authored is an accuracy defect in the one field whose whole job is accuracy, and the
schema parser cannot catch a provenance lie, only a missing key.

Output: a new `none-flat-authored` provenance value threaded through the area config, the graph
builder, its test and the normative schema doc.
</objective>
