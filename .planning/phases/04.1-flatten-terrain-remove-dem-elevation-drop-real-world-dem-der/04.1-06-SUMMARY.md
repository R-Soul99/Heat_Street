## Decisions Made
- Strengthened the provenance-lie test beyond the plan's literal spec (see key-decisions above) — the substring checks the plan specified (`3DEP`/`USGS`/`Copernicus`) do not, by themselves, catch a copy-paste of the real USGS attribution VALUE, since that value doesn't literally contain those acronyms.
