<objective>
Decouple building ground-seating from the DEM by replacing `buildingBoxes`'s
`ElevationSampler` (lat/lon) parameter with a local-ENU ground-height function, so buildings sit
on whatever terrain the compiler actually produces.

Purpose: `tools/map-compiler/geometry/building-box.ts` is the last compiler stage besides the
elevation and heightfield stages that reads the DEM. Until it stops, `sources/dem.ts` cannot be
deleted. Seating buildings on the compiled relief grid also preserves plan 04-11's building
grounding fix (`author/collision.ts` extends a building collider's bottom face down to the local
terrain) without change.

Output: a `GroundHeightSampler`-based `buildingBoxes` and updated test fixtures in its three
consumer suites.
</objective>
