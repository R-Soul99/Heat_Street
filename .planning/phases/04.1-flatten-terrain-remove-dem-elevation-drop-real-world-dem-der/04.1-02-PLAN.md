  <action>
Change `TARGET_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` from `20` to `6` in `src/core/road-geometry.ts`. Leave
`MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` at `3` and `SHOULDER_BUILDING_SAFETY_MARGIN_M` at `2` unchanged.

Extend (do not replace) the constant's existing doc comment, following this file's own
"measured-then-revised, with the measurement cited" convention. The new paragraph must record:

- WHY 20m existed: `HEIGHTFIELD_SINK_M` was 4.5m and was subtracted from every sampled height,
  so the road-to-terrain gap sat near 5m almost everywhere (measured average 5.09m); 20m was
  what kept that common gap under ~14 degrees.
- WHY 6m is now correct: under D-01 roads are at y=0 and under D-02b off-road relief fades to
  exactly 0 at the paved edge. The gap the ramp bridges is therefore no longer the sink — it is
  only whatever residual relief the coarse heightfield interpolates near the road. Bound it
  explicitly: the compiled grid is 128 cells over ~5.5km, i.e. ~43m spacing, so the nearest grid
  node to any road is at most ~30m away and carries at most
  `RELIEF_AMPLITUDE_M * smoothstep(30 / RELIEF_FALLOFF_DISTANCE_M)` = `2 * smoothstep(30/130)`
  = ~0.27m of relief; add `HEIGHTFIELD_SINK_M` (0.1m) and the worst-case shoulder height delta
  is ~0.37m. Over a 6m shoulder that is ~3.5 degrees, and even at the building-clamped
  `MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` (3m) it is ~7 degrees — against the 12.9 degree median and 26.9 degree
  p95 measured on the DEM-era 20m shoulder.
- The bracketing failure modes (this file's required convention): raising it back toward 20m
  reintroduces the "ridiculous... extra road width" defect this phase exists to fix; lowering it
  below `MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` would invert the clamp in `resolvePointShoulderWidth` and is
  forbidden by the invariant `MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M <= TARGET_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M`.
- The standing `[ASSUMED]` tag and an explicit note that this value is flagged for re-judging in
  plan 04.1-10's D-06 drive-every-road session, and that shoulders render with the ROAD's own
  surface material (see `groupBySurface` in `tools/map-compiler/author/gltf.ts`) — so if 6m of
  road-coloured verge still reads as "extra road width" during that session, the options are a
  further width reduction or reassigning the shoulder surface, and that choice belongs to the
  session, not to a speculative change here.

Also add, immediately below the three constants, an exported compile-time-documented invariant
comment stating `MIN_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M <= TARGET_SHOULDER_WIDTH_M` and naming
`tests/road-geometry.test.ts` as its enforcer. Do not add a runtime assertion — this module is
on the per-frame-adjacent path and the repo's convention is a mirroring test, not a guard.

Make no structural change to `buildRibbon`, `buildEdgeShoulder`, `buildRoadShoulders`,
`buildJunctionFan` or `buildRoadGeometry` — they are elevation-source-agnostic and stay as-is.
