## Ragged Road-Edge Finding — Detail

Three screenshots at three locations show the same signature: the paved road's edge silhouette zigzags rather than running straight/smoothly curved, distinct from (and screenshotted separately from) the road-texture-missing gap in step 1.

| Location | Distance to nearest crest centre | Nearest crest's `nearestPavedEdge` (plan 04.1-09) |
|---|---|---|
| (-420.0, 102.2) | 0m (at `main-street-rise` itself) | 36.5m |
| (-1227.2, -1062.3) | 0m (at `south-approach` itself) | 36.5m |
| (-384.66, 229.75) | ~132m from `main-street-rise` | 36.5m |

`north-ridge` (51.5m paved-edge clearance) shows no such defect anywhere it was driven. The two affected crests share an identical, smaller 36.5m clearance figure (from plan 04.1-09's own per-crest measurement script) — a real, falsifiable lead, though the third location (~132m out, just past `RELIEF_FALLOFF_DISTANCE_M`'s 130m radius) means proximity to a crest is not the whole story. The zigzag reads as a road/shoulder-edge POLYLINE generation defect (the paved outline itself is crooked), not a terrain-poking-through-road blend issue — `HEIGHTFIELD_SINK_M` governs the latter, not the former, which is why Task 2 did not attempt a sink retune here. Most likely candidates for a future investigation: `src/core/road-geometry.ts`'s or `src/core/shoulder-clearance.ts`'s per-point offset/clamp math (both introduced/reworked in Phase 04-11, now exercised under this phase's changed shoulder width and relief), sampling off-road relief noise into the edge offset itself.
