## Decisions Made

- Dome triangle winding: derived from first principles rather than assumed from `road-geometry.ts`'s ribbon precedent, since the radial (apex-centered, angle-parameterized) topology is structurally different from a ribbon strip. Both the apex fan and the ring-to-ring quad strips wind CW from +Y under the naive (increasing-angle) vertex order; every emitted triangle swaps its second/third index to correct this, confirmed by the CCW test.
- Task 2's radius-containment test (`buildCrestGeometry` item 12) uses a synthetic near-origin `AuthoredCrest` fixture instead of `JULIETTE_GA_CRESTS`'s real ~(-780, 263) / ~(-1227, -1062) coordinates, because `Float32Array` position storage loses precision proportional to coordinate magnitude (~1e-5 at that range), which fails the plan's literal `radiusM + 1e-6` tolerance for reasons unrelated to geometry correctness — the same precision floor `tests/road-geometry.test.ts` avoids by keeping its own fixtures near the origin.
- `buildRoadDistanceField`'s bounding-box reject formula keeps BOTH the box-expansion-by-`halfWidthM` (at construction) AND the trailing `- halfWidthM` (in the per-query formula), exactly as the plan specifies. Empirical stress-testing found only the SECOND (formula-level) subtraction is provably redundant for correctness — removing just that term still passes the brute-force-equivalence test, confirmed by direct cross-product-style inequality analysis (the pre-expanded box distance is always a valid, merely looser, lower bound). Removing `halfWidthM` from the box EXPANSION, however, does break the test (verified, then reverted) — that is the genuinely load-bearing half of the optimization. Shipped code matches the plan's literal spec since the extra subtraction costs nothing and is explicitly requested.
