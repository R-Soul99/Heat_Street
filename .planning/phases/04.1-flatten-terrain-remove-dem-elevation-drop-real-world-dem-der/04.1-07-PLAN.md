Ship D-04b's authored crests into both compiled artifacts and the runtime physics world, using
the same "one algorithm, two consumers" wiring `buildRoadShoulders` already uses.

Purpose: `src/core/crest-geometry.ts` (plan 04.1-03) produces the geometry, but nothing consumes
it yet. Until the `.glb` carries a crest mesh and `map-scene.ts` builds crest colliders, D-04b's
"launch over the hill" moment does not exist in the game.

Output: a `crests` mesh in the compiled `.glb`, one trimesh collider per crest at runtime, and
tests for both.
