**Two findings from 02-RESEARCH.md that invert the priorities recorded in 02-CONTEXT.md, and
which you should know before judging the feel:**
1. **D-08's rollover risk does not exist.** Full lock at 60 mph AND at 110 mph both produced
   at most 1.5 degrees of tilt with no assist at all. The anti-roll/downforce knob shipped
   with a default of ZERO, deliberately. It is retained for Phase 3's surfaces and Phase 4's
   uneven terrain.
2. **D-06's body roll is the genuinely hard part, and the assist that produces it is the most
   unstable code in the phase.** Rapier hardcodes `roll_influence = 0.1` and does not expose
   it, so lateral tire forces generate almost no roll moment: 1.5-2 degrees at 0.85 g where a
   real car rolls 4-6. The body-roll assist at gain 0.10 gives 5.46 degrees; at 0.20 it flips
   the car onto its roof. Its slider is capped at 0.15 and there is a hard CI gate asserting
   no routine ever exceeds 15 degrees of tilt.
Also note: **power oversteer is not emergent.** Throttle cannot break the rear loose at any
engine force, so the throttle half of SC1 is an authored `powerOversteerGain` term with a
starting guess of 0.5 that was never swept. Expect to tune it.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Run `npm run dev` and open the URL WITH `?debug` appended. Press `KeyG` for the tuning panel
and `KeyT` for the telemetry panel.

**SC5 part A — retune live, no code edit.**
1. Drag sliders in the Grip and Assists folders and confirm the car's behaviour changes
   IMMEDIATELY, mid-drive, with no reload and no rebuild.
2. Drag the `mass` slider and confirm the change applies on release (not during the drag) and
   does not launch or teleport the car.
3. Press `KeyT`, press `Run telemetry suite`, and confirm the results table reflects YOUR
   current values, not the committed defaults — change `frictionSlip` noticeably and re-run
   to see the skidpad number move.
4. Reload the page and confirm your values survived. Then press `Reset to defaults`, confirm
   the label becomes `Click again to confirm`, click again, and confirm the values return to
   the committed baseline.
5. While the tuning panel is open, click into a numeric field and TYPE the letters `g` and
   `t`. Neither panel may toggle. (This is the focus guard from plan 02-05.)

**SC1 — the slide is readable and recoverable.**
6. Get to about 60 mph, turn, and hold `Space`. The rear should step out into a large,
   held, speed-scrubbing slide. Release `Space` and steer INTO the slide. It should catch.
7. Repeat with throttle instead of handbrake — full throttle plus steering lock. If the rear
   does not step out at all, raise `powerOversteerGain`. If it snaps instantly, lower it.
8. Brake hard WHILE turning. The car should push wide and understeer rather than simply
   stopping shorter. If there is no penalty, `frictionSlip` is too high — the useful band is
   0.6 to 2.0 and the dial is completely dead above about 10.
9. Judge: is the slide READABLE — can you see it coming and see it happening — and is it
   RECOVERABLE rather than terminal? That judgement is SC1 and only you can make it.

**SC2 — steering.**
10. Drive with the keyboard only. It must not feel binary or snap to lock. If it feels
    sluggish, raise `steerRampPerSec`; if twitchy, lower it. If counter-steer feels late,
    raise `steerReturnPerSec`.
11. Connect a gamepad, press a button so the browser exposes it, and drive. Steering must be
    proportional to stick deflection with no ramp-in, and the triggers must be analog. If the
    axes are wrong or swapped, say so — 02-RESEARCH.md flags the standard-mapping indices as
    an unverified assumption (A1) and it is a one-line fix.

**SC5 part B — the Bullitt sign-off.**
12. Drive for a real session, not thirty seconds. Reference: Bullitt's Mustang chase — heavy,
    momentum-driven, hard body roll through corners, weight you have to plan around.
13. Watch specifically for: does the nose DIVE under braking? Does the body ROLL visibly
    through a corner? Does the car feel like it has mass you must respect, or like a go-kart?
    Body roll is the priority per D-06 — raise `bodyRollGain` toward its 0.15 cap if the car
    reads flat, and lower `suspensionStiffness` for more dive and squat.
14. When you are happy, press `KeyT` and `Run telemetry suite` one final time. Report the six
    numbers.
15. Report the final slider values for every knob you changed. Task 2 commits them.

If any routine FAILS at your chosen values, say so explicitly with the number — the D-14
bands (6.0-7.0 s to 60 mph, 110-135 ft to stop) are a locked decision, so a failure means
either the tuning needs another pass or the band needs an explicit decision to move.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| session values -> committed defaults | Hand-transcribed numbers become the baseline every future phase and every medal time depends on |
| tuning panel -> `localStorage` | The session's working values persist in a user-editable blob |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-22 | Tampering | transcription error moving a default outside its tested band | mitigate | The full telemetry suite is re-run against the committed defaults as a gate, and `tests/vehicle-tuning.test.ts`'s recursive `min <= default <= max` check (plan 02-01) fails if any transcribed value falls outside `TUNING_RANGES`. |
| T-02-12 | Denial of Service | a session value pushing the body-roll assist past its stability cliff | mitigate | `TUNING_RANGES.assists.bodyRollGain.max` is 0.15, below the measured 0.20 flip threshold, so the panel cannot produce such a value; the `roll assist stability` CI gate re-runs against the new defaults and fails above 15 degrees of tilt in any routine. |
| T-02-01 | Tampering / DoS | the session's persisted blob outliving the session | accept | The blob is still validated by `parseSavedTuning` at every boot; a stale session value is a developer-experience concern, not a security one. `Reset to defaults` clears it. |
| T-02-23 | Repudiation | a perceptual sign-off with no record | mitigate | The checkpoint requires the developer to report final knob values and the six telemetry numbers, and the SUMMARY records before/after for every change plus any research claim the session contradicted.
