## The idea

At extreme heat/wanted levels, pursuers could escalate beyond police to
military-grade response (armored vehicles, possibly aircraft). Explicitly
flagged by the player as "undoubtedly fun to try" but also a possible tonal
stretch beyond the 60s-70s muscle-car chase-cinema theme, and a real
handling-mismatch concern: muscle cars aren't built to evade military
hardware, so the escape design (what's the learnable counter-play?) needs
real thought, not just "bigger pursuer."

Loosely coupled to a second idea from the same conversation: a larger
vehicle roster in general (beyond the 3-5-car v2 plan already in
REQUIREMENTS.md VEH-05). If pursuit escalates to military-tier threats, the
player may want/need access to more capable evasion vehicles, which pushes
vehicle variety scope further than currently planned.

## Why deferred

- v1 and v2 are already scoped around heat tiers 1-5, all police-only
  (GET-03 in v1, GET-05 in v2). Military escalation is a tier beyond what's
  currently designed or research-backed — FEATURES.md's NFS Most Wanted
  reference tops out at Rhino SUVs and helicopters, not military hardware.
- Risks diluting the core identity (Bullitt/Starsky & Hutch cop-chase
  cinema) if not handled carefully — worth validating that heat tiers 1-5
  land well before deciding whether tier 6+ pulls the tone somewhere new
  or just extends the same fantasy further (e.g. Smokey and the Bandit /
  Cannonball Run already flirt with over-the-top escalation, so there's
  tonal precedent either way).
