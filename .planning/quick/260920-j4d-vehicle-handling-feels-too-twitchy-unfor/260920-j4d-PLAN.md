<objective>
Re-tune the already-tuned handling constants so the car is more FORGIVING without becoming
less loose. Two live symptoms reported by the developer:

1. **Accelerating out of a slide does not recover quickly enough.** Applying throttle while
   counter-steering out of a slide should straighten the car; today it prolongs the slide.
2. **Small steering inputs at speed snowball.** A slight tap grows into an increasing
   left-right drift and then a spin-out.

Purpose: `.planning/STATE.md` records the Phase 02-07 and 02-10 sessions that set
`rearSideFriction 0.2`, `powerOversteerGain 1.1`, `bodyRollGain 0.12` and
`slideCatchGain 0.12`. This is a RE-tune of those same knobs with documented history, not a
first pass — and the two symptoms above point at a specific, code-derived mechanism (see
`<root_cause>`), not at a vague "needs more grip".

Output: two new headless diagnostic routines that make both symptoms measurable, a bounded
retune of at most four constants, and a human sign-off in the browser.

NON-GOALS (explicit — do not do these):
- Do NOT "fix" this by cranking grip up until the car stops sliding. The RWD-loose feel is
  this project's stated core value. `rearSideFriction` is the LAST lever, not the first, and
  must not exceed 0.26 in this plan (it is 0.2 today; `frontSideFriction` is 1.0, so even 0.26
  leaves the rear ~4x looser than the front).
- Do NOT change `drive.maxSteerLock`. `tests/vehicle-tuning.test.ts:75` asserts it is exactly
  `Math.PI / 4`, and `routines.ts`'s `STEER_FRACTION_15DEG` and `HANDBRAKE_STEER_FRACTION`
  both explicitly document that they assume that value — moving it silently invalidates the
  documented steer angle of two existing routines.
- Do NOT change any `TELEMETRY_TARGETS` band for the six existing routines. D-14's bands
  (6.0-7.0 s accel, 110-135 ft brake, 0.75-1.05 g skidpad, the 15 deg roll-stability cutoff)
  are a locked decision; a value that breaks one of them is the wrong value, not a reason to
  move the band.
- Do NOT change any `TUNING_RANGES` bound. If a candidate value needs a wider range, that is
  the signal to pick a different candidate.
- Do NOT touch `src/core/surface-tuning.ts`. The six surface `lateralGrip`/`forwardGrip`
  profiles were human-signed-off in Phase 03-12 and the baseline retune propagates to them by
  composition (`vehicle.ts` step 4: `rearSfs * rearLateralGrip[i]`) — which is exactly why
  `tests/surface-telemetry.test.ts` is a required re-verify below, not an optional one.
- Do NOT add the two new routines to the `ROUTINES` array. They are diagnostics for this
  retune, not new members of the canonical six that `runAllRoutines` and the browser panel
  iterate.

<root_cause>
Read these facts off the shipped code before picking any value. They are derived, not assumed,
and they are what makes this a bounded retune rather than a blind sweep.

**Fact 1 — `powerOversteerGain` above 1.0 does nothing at full lock.**
`src/physics/vehicle.ts` step 4:

    rearSfs = handbrake ? handbrakeRearSideFriction
                        : lerp(rearSideFriction, handbrakeRearSideFriction,
                               clamp(powerOversteerGain * throttle * |steer|, 0, 1))

At `throttle = 1, |steer| = 1` the clamp saturates at 1.0 for ANY gain >= 1.0, so gain 1.0 and
gain 1.1 produce byte-identical rear grip there. Everything the 0.1 above 1.0 buys is spent on
PARTIAL inputs — i.e. exactly the small-steering-input oversensitivity now being reported.
(Phase 02-10's recorded finding that "1.0 produced none, 1.1 gave a readable step-out" cannot
have been a full-lock observation for this reason; it was most likely measured before or
during the same session's `rearSideFriction 0.12 -> 0.2` change and is confounded by it.)
Consequence: `1.1 -> 1.0` is a strictly free reduction in partial-input looseness.

**Fact 2 — the authored oversteer term cannot tell provocation from correction.**
The lerp factor is `gain * throttle * |steer|` and `|steer|` is UNSIGNED. Counter-steering out
of a slide under throttle has a large `|steer|` and a large `throttle`, so it drives rear grip
DOWN to the handbrake value at precisely the moment the driver is asking for it back. That is
symptom 1, mechanically. Reducing the gain is the constants-only lever against it.

**Fact 3 — the existing six routines are blind to both symptoms.**
- `slalom` runs at 50 mph with `SLALOM_STEER_FRACTION = 0.5` (22.5 deg) — not a small input.
- `handbrake` measures recovery at `throttle: 0.2`, near-coast, which is exactly the regime
  where the `throttle * |steer|` term is near-inert. It never measures a POWER-ON recovery.
So "all six still pass" proves no regression, and proves nothing about the fix. Task 1 exists
to close that gap.

**Fact 4 — `slideCatchDamping` is the untouched knob.**
`vehicle-assists.ts` slide-catch block: `torque.y = (-slip * kp - w.y * kd) * DT`, where
`kp = slideCatchGain * I.y` and `kd = slideCatchGain * slideCatchDamping * I.y`. The reported
"increasing left-right drift" is an under-damped yaw oscillation; `kd` is the term that damps
it. `slideCatchDamping` is 0.35 in a `0..2` range and has never been swept in any recorded
session. Raising it increases yaw-RATE damping without raising the slip-angle restoring term,
so it fights divergence more than it flattens a held drift.

**Fact 5 — `slideCatchGain 0.3` was rejected for a reason that no longer applies.**
STATE.md, Phase 02-10: 0.3 was rejected because it made the high-speed straight-line spin
trigger EARLIER. That spin was separately root-caused to `rearSideFriction` and fixed, and
STATE.md records the carry-forward as RESOLVED in Phase 3 (03-06's headless sweep plus
03-12's hands-on drive across all six surfaces). `slideCatchGain` is therefore legitimately
re-sweepable now that its confounder is gone.

**Fact 6 — telemetry bypasses the steering ramp.**
`steerRampPerSec` / `steerReturnPerSec` live in `src/input/live-input.ts`, not in
`vehicle.tick`, which consumes `frame.steer` raw. A routine's steer pulse is therefore applied
instantaneously at the wheel — a harsher input than a real keyboard tap, which is fine for a
probe but means these two knobs cannot be evaluated headlessly. They stay a task-3
panel-only lever.
</root_cause>
