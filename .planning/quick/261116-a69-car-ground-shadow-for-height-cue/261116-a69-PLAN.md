# Quick task 261116-a69: Car ground shadow

<objective>
Make the player's car always show a readable shadow on the ground beneath it in the game, so that on
sparse terrain and in the air the player can see where the car is and how high it is.
</objective>

<user_words>
"car also needs a shadow, against sparse backgrounds at the wrong angle i can't see any definition or
idea how high i am."
</user_words>

<context>
- The renderer already enables shadow maps (`src/render/renderer.ts`), the chassis and wheels cast
  shadows and a sun light casts them (`src/render/vehicle-view.ts`), and the terrain mesh receives
  shadows (`src/render/terrain-view.ts`). The user still sees no usable shadow on terrain maps such
  as `?map=demo-hills`, so first find out why.
- The camera is a permanent high-angle helicopter view at mid to far distance.
- Frame budget: `src/core/frame-budget.ts` and `docs/frame-budget.md`. Draw calls on demo-hills peak
  at about 70 of 80 while driving, so the fix may add at most ONE draw call and no extra shadow pass.
</context>

<tasks>

<task id="1" name="Diagnose the missing sun shadow">
Read how the sun light and its shadow camera are set up and where they sit relative to the car on
large terrain maps (shadow frustum size and position, whether it follows the car, bias, map size,
whether terrain materials/chunks actually receive). Write the root cause into the SUMMARY. If it is
a contained fix that stays inside the frame budget (for example the shadow frustum is fixed around
the origin and needs to follow the car each frame, snapped to texels to avoid shimmer), fix it. If
fixing it properly is not contained, leave the sun shadow as it is and say so.
</task>

<task id="2" name="Ground contact shadow under the car">
Add a soft dark contact shadow directly beneath the car, independent of the sun angle:
- One flat quad with a radial-gradient alpha texture generated in code (no asset file), slightly
  larger than the car footprint, oriented to the car's yaw.
- Positioned each frame at the ground point straight below the chassis and laid on the ground normal
  there, using the terrain height sampler the render side already has (or a single downward physics
  ray if that is what the render layer uses elsewhere). It must also land on ramps/crests, scenery
  buildings' roofs are not required.
- As the car rises it grows a little and fades: full strength on the ground, clearly visible at 2 to
  10 m, faint but still visible up to about 30 m. Put the height-to-size/opacity curve in a pure,
  unit-tested function.
- No z-fighting with terrain or road paint (polygon offset or a small lift), no shadow casting or
  receiving on the quad itself, depthWrite off, render-interpolated with the car so it does not
  jitter against the fixed physics step.
- Applies to the player car in every mode and map type. Pursuer/AI cars: only if it is the same code
  path at no extra draw call per car (for example one instanced mesh); otherwise player only.
- Dispose it with the vehicle view.
Do not touch physics, vehicle handling, tuning values or the camera.
</task>

<task id="3" name="Reference grid off on terrain maps">
The user: "the grid that's on all the maps is increasingly irrelevant and doesn't look very nice."
`createVehicleView` in `src/render/vehicle-view.ts` always builds a flat 10 m reference grid at
y = 0.01 (`buildReferenceGrid`), and `src/main.ts` bounds it to the terrain extents. On terrain maps
it is a flat plane that only shows where the ground is at or below zero.
- Do not add the reference grid on maps that have sculpted terrain (a terrain sidecar / heightfield).
- Leave it exactly as it is on flat maps without terrain (the flat proving-ground maps use it as the
  only speed reference).
- Add a URL override, validated like the other map-select parameters: `?grid=1` forces it on,
  `?grid=0` forces it off, on any map. Pure parse function with tests; document it where the other
  URL parameters are documented.
One commit.
</task>

</tasks>
