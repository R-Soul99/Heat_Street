# Quick 261116-a69: Car ground shadow, sun shadow fix, grid off on terrain

## Root cause of the missing sun shadow
`createVehicleView` built the sun's shadow camera as a fixed 60 m x 60 m box (SHADOW_AREA_HALF = 30)
centred on the world origin (sun at (20,30,20), target at the origin) and nothing ever moved it. The
car only cast a shadow within about 30 m of (0,0), so on any large terrain map it cast none. Bias,
map size and receivers were fine (terrain chunks do receive).

## What changed
1. `VehicleView.followShadow(x,y,z)` moves sun and target with the car each frame, with the window
   centre snapped to shadow texels in light space (`src/render/shadow-follow.ts`, pure, tested). Same
   single shadow pass, no extra draws.
2. Contact shadow: a car shadow (`src/render/car-shadow.ts`) already existed but only on the flat
   proving grounds, as three draws (patch, outline, stem) and fading to nothing by about 10 m. Now it
   is on every map as ONE quad draw, lies on the ground normal (new read-only
   `createGroundSampler` in `src/physics/ground-probe.ts`, one `castRayAndGetNormal` per frame), turns
   with the car, and uses the pure tested `shadowLook(air)` curve: full on the ground, 0.49 opacity at
   2 m, 0.26 at 10 m, 0.13 at 30 m and beyond, scale capped at 3.5x. Player car only. Frame budget doc
   updated (+1 draw).
3. `?grid=1|0` override (`parseGridOverride`, `shouldShowReferenceGrid` in `src/core/map-select.ts`,
   tested, documented in their JSDoc as the other URL params are; there is no separate URL-param doc).
   `createVehicleView` gained `showGrid`.

## Choices made for you
- Outline and stem kept only on the flat proving grounds, as before; terrain maps get the patch alone,
  to stay within "at most one added draw call".
- The grid stays ON for flat proving grounds even though feel-test loads in terrain format, since the
  plan says they use it as their only speed reference. It is off on other terrain maps (demo-hills,
  arena-test, popp-roads, juliette-ga, grid-test if terrain).
- Pursuer/AI cars get no shadow (would be one draw each).
- The old `createGroundProbe` is left in place, now unused by main.
- The patch is not disposed on teardown because main.ts has no teardown path for the view either
  (the shadow object has `dispose()`).
